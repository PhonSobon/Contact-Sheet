Remove Watermark

Remove watermarks from PDFs — text, watermark layers, stamps and scanned pages — or from photos and screenshots by painting over them.

Drop a PDF here, or click to choose one
.PDF · UP TO 40MB · UP TO 400 PAGES
Found in this PDF
Watermark text
Only text that is entirely the watermark is removed — a sentence that merely contains the same word is kept.
Scanned pages
For PDFs made from scans or photos, where the watermark is part of the page picture. The pale option keeps dark text; the colour option is for coloured stamps (red, blue…).
Drop images here, or click to choose files
.PNG · .JPG · .WEBP · UP TO 10 IMAGES · 20MB EACH
Paint over the watermark — the marked area (red) is filled in from its surroundings.
Automatic cleanup (all images)
Pale: fades light grey or coloured watermarks on documents, keeping dark text. Colour: choose Pick colour and click the watermark. Painted marks and automatic cleanup can be combined.
Use this only on files you own or have permission to edit.
